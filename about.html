About Wisdom Traders

Most packaging factories won't take an order under a few thousand pieces. That leaves home bakers, new clothing brands and single-branch restaurants without a supplier. They end up buying from whoever has stock that week, at whatever price.

Wisdom Traders buys in factory quantities and sells in the quantities small businesses actually use: fifty pizza boxes, two hundred mailers, one roll of bubble wrap. Every item has a printed price for each quantity, so you know the cost before you message us.

How an order works

Add items to the order list on this site, or just send us a WhatsApp message with what you need. We check stock, confirm the total and the delivery charge, and dispatch once you confirm. In Karachi, cash on delivery is fine for stock items.

For printed packaging, such as boxes with your logo or custom-size cartons, we work with printers we've used for years. We send you a proof before anything is printed.
