# Wireframe brief: Wisdom Traders website

## How to use this brief
- Make **low-fidelity, greyscale wireframes**: boxes, lines and real text only.
- No colours, fonts, logos, icons, photos or styling. If you need an image, draw a box with an X and label it, e.g. "product image".
- **Don't look at the current site or any competitor before you finish.** We want your own take on structure and flow, not a reaction to ours.
- Use real content from this brief, not lorem ipsum. Long product names and big numbers break layouts, and we want to see that happen.
- Any tool is fine: paper, Figma, Balsamiq, Excalidraw.

## The business
Wisdom Traders is a packaging wholesaler in Karachi. It sells stock boxes, bags, bottles, stickers and packing supplies in small-to-medium quantities (from about 50 pieces). It also takes orders for packaging printed with the customer's logo.

**Customers:** small businesses such as pizzerias, home bakers, cafés, skincare and clothing brands, and online sellers. Most are in Karachi, some elsewhere in Pakistan. Many reorder every few weeks.

**How they browse:** mostly on phones, often on slow mobile data. Many prefer WhatsApp to forms or email.

## Constraints (not up for debate)
1. **No online payment.** The customer builds an order list on the site and sends it to us as a WhatsApp message. We confirm stock and the delivery charge, then they pay (cash on delivery in Karachi, or bank transfer / Easypaisa / JazzCash).
2. **Every product has quantity-based pricing.** Example for a 12" pizza box:

   | Quantity | Price each |
   |---|---|
   | 50–99 | Rs 64 |
   | 100–499 | Rs 56 |
   | 500–999 | Rs 49 |
   | 1,000+ | Rs 44 |

3. **Every product has a minimum order quantity**, and the customer must not be able to order less than it.
4. **Delivery** takes 1–2 working days in Karachi and 3–5 working days elsewhere in Pakistan.
5. **Printed orders** need a minimum of 500 pieces (50 for stickers), take 7–10 working days, and are paid 100% in advance.
6. **Design phone-first.** Show every page at 390px wide first, then at desktop width (about 1440px).

## What a visitor needs to be able to do (in priority order)
1. Work out within a few seconds what we sell and whether we handle small orders.
2. Find a specific item, by browsing or by searching (e.g. "12 inch pizza box", "jar").
3. See the price at the quantity they want, and how much they'd save by ordering more.
4. Build a list of several items with quantities and send it to us on WhatsApp.
5. Ask for a quote for printed or custom-size packaging.
6. Find out about delivery times, delivery charges and payment methods.
7. Contact us directly (WhatsApp, phone, email, address).
8. Decide whether we're trustworthy enough to send money to.

## Content inventory

### Product categories (9)
Food & restaurant boxes · Mailer & product boxes · Shipping cartons · Bags, pouches & flyers · Bottles & jars · Stickers, labels & tags · Tissues & napkins · Packing accessories · Money envelopes

### What a product has
- Name, e.g. "Kraft bag with twisted handles, large". Some names are long.
- Size, e.g. 12 × 10 × 8 in.
- Material, e.g. "E-flute corrugated, kraft outside, white inside".
- Unit sold: piece, pack, roll, kg or box.
- Minimum order quantity.
- 3–4 price breaks.
- Whether it can be printed with a logo.
- An image: photos are not available yet, so plan a placeholder.

There are 42 products in total. Categories hold between 2 and 11 products each.

### Other content
- A list of about 12 customer businesses, each with a name, business type and city. No logos are available.
- Delivery and payment terms (above).
- How a printing order works: send the request, get a price and proof, approve and pay the advance, then we print and deliver.
- Contact details: WhatsApp, phone, email, warehouse address, opening hours (Mon–Sat, 10am–7pm).
- A short About text explaining why we exist: factories won't take small orders, so we buy in bulk and sell in small quantities.

## Screens to wireframe
1. **Home**
2. **Catalogue**, showing all products and one category view
3. **Product page**
4. **Order list**, including the customer's details and the send step
5. **Printing quote request**
6. **Contact**
7. **About** (optional)

## States to show
These are required, not optional:
- Search with no results.
- An empty order list.
- A quantity below the minimum on a product page and in the order list.
- A form submitted with required fields missing or a bad phone number.
- What the customer sees after they tap "send on WhatsApp".
- A product name long enough to wrap onto 3 lines on a phone.
- A category with only 2 products.

## Deliverable
- Each screen at mobile width, and at desktop width where the layout changes.
- Short annotations explaining the reasoning: why this is first, why this is hidden, and so on.
- One user flow: landing on the home page → finding a 12" pizza box → ordering 500 → adding tape → sending on WhatsApp.
- A list of anything in this brief you think is wrong, missing or unnecessary.

## Questions we'd like your opinion on
- What should a first-time visitor see first on a phone?
- How should quantity-based pricing appear in a list of products, as opposed to on the product page?
- Should the order list be its own page, a slide-out panel, or something else?
- How do we earn trust without real photos, reviews or customer logos yet?
- Does the printing / custom-order path deserve as much space as stock ordering?
